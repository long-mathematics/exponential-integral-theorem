# v7 language and presentation revision

## Scope

This revision is a language, organization, and presentation pass over v6. It does not replace the preceding mathematical audits or introduce a new proof strategy.

## Abstract

- Rewritten to lead with the relative exponential Nori--Ayoub theorem, the fixed-part theorem, the Galois exact sequence, and functional period injectivity.
- Removed the earlier emphasis on rank-one saturation. Differential characters now appear only in one sentence describing the proof method.
- Replaced prose such as “algebraic nonzero Fourier scale” by the precise notation `\lambda\in\overline{\mathbf Q}^{\times}`.
- Distinguished functional relations from arithmetic relations created by specialization in one concise closing sentence.

## Introduction

- Removed the redundant phrase “of characteristic zero” after “subfield of `\mathbf C`.”
- Reorganized the main-results section around four headline results:
  1. the exponentiated perverse-Nori heart;
  2. the invariant fixed-part theorem;
  3. relative exponential Galois exactness;
  4. the relative exponential Nori--Ayoub theorem.
- Moved rank-one saturation out of the headline presentation and retained it only as technical machinery in the body.
- Presented the canonical scale `\lambda=1` first in the functional theorem, with the algebraic-scale extension stated afterwards.
- Condensed the proof overview to the three essential inputs: Sabbah, Lam--Litt, and Huber--Wüstholz.

## Whole-manuscript language audit

- Removed draft-history and audit vocabulary, including “repaired,” “verified,” “sanity check,” “load-bearing,” “interface,” and theorem-ledger language.
- Removed defensive corrections to claims no longer made.
- Consolidated repeated Fourier-scale and specialization caveats.
- Removed redundant statements of automatic implications while retaining short implications that are used in proofs.
- Renamed sections and remarks to describe mathematical content rather than the history of the proof.
- Recast the applications section as examples under stated hypotheses rather than broad standalone claims.
- Rewrote the conclusion to summarize the theorem rather than the proof-development path.
- Shortened the AI-assisted research disclosure.

## Authorship

The manuscript continues to list Christopher D. Long and Antoine-Auguste Le Blanc as authors and retains Le Blanc's cryptographic author identifier.
