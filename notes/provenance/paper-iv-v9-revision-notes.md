# Revision notes: v9 Gamma functional presentation

This revision starts from the collision-proof v7 source and replaces the brief cyclotomic Gamma example by an explicit complete functional presentation.

## Added results

### Functional cyclotomic Gamma presentation

For a finite packet

\[
G_j(z)=N\int_0^\infty x^{m_j-1}e^{-zx^N}\,dx
      =\Gamma(m_j/N)z^{-m_j/N},
\]

the paper now computes the complete kernel of

\[
C_\Gamma(z)[X_1,\ldots,X_s]\longrightarrow
\mathscr M(\widetilde{\mathbb G_m}),
\qquad X_j\mapsto G_j(z),
\]

where \(C_\Gamma\) is the actual field generated by the chosen Gamma constants.  The kernel is the Kummer-weight binomial ideal

\[
\left(
\gamma^{\mathbf b}X^{\mathbf a}
-z^{-q}\gamma^{\mathbf a}X^{\mathbf b}:
M(\mathbf a)-M(\mathbf b)=qN
\right).
\]

### Complete fixed-denominator packet

For the packet \(1\le m<N\), the complete ideal is generated by

\[
\gamma_1^mX_m-\gamma_mX_1^m\quad(2\le m<N),
\qquad
X_1^N-\gamma_1^Nz^{-1}.
\]

An explicit denominator-three example is included.

## Proof audit

The proof was checked at the following points.

1. **Gamma integral identity.** The substitution \(u=zx^N\) is made first for positive real \(z\), where it is literal.  Holomorphy on the right half-plane and analytic continuation then give the identity on the universal cover.  This avoids an unjustified contour rotation for complex \(z\).
2. **Kummer field extension.** On the universal cover, \(t=z^{-1/N}\).  Since \(\mu_N\subset C_\Gamma\), the action \(t\mapsto\zeta t\) has fixed field \(C_\Gamma(t^N)=C_\Gamma(z)\).  Hence \(1,t,\ldots,t^{N-1}\) is a basis.
3. **Kernel inclusion.** Every displayed binomial vanishes because equal weights modulo \(N\) differ by a power of \(z^{-1}=t^N\).
4. **Kernel exhaustion.** An arbitrary relation is decomposed by the residue of its Kummer weight modulo \(N\).  Linear independence of the Kummer basis forces each residue coefficient to vanish, and the binomials reduce every monomial in that residue class to one chosen representative.
5. **Finite generating set for the full packet.** The first family eliminates \(X_m\) in favor of \(X_1\); linear independence of \(1,t,\ldots,t^{N-1}\) proves injectivity of the remaining quotient.

The explicit kernel formula is elementary and appears not to have been recorded in this Gamma-specific form.  It is presented as an explicit example, not as the main novelty of the paper.

## Rohrlich--Lang boundary

A new remark distinguishes the result from the classical conjectures.

- Rohrlich predicts that multiplicative algebraic relations among rational Gamma values and \(2\pi i\) are generated by the standard translation, reflection, and multiplication identities.
- Lang strengthens this to all polynomial algebraic relations.
- The new functional theorem works over the actual constant field \(C_\Gamma\), so those constant relations have already been imposed.
- The functional variable contributes only the explicit Kummer binomials.  The unresolved arithmetic problem remains the relation theory of the constant Gamma values.

The manuscript therefore describes the result as a fixed-denominator functional analogue of the Rohrlich--Lang picture, without claiming progress on the classical numerical conjecture itself.
