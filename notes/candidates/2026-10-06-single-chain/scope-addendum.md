# Scope addendum to the independent audit

Date: 6 October 2026.

The frozen scope paragraph should not imply that failure of (Q) necessarily forces a smaller differential Galois group. The homogeneous-group proof uses (Q) only to ensure that the ordered differences c_i-c_j, i!=j, are nonzero and pairwise distinct. That finite distinct-difference condition suffices for the same torus/root-space/component-group argument. No other step in the homogeneous irreducibility proof uses rational independence.

For example, let f'(x)=x(x-1)(x-2)(x-4) and choose

f(x)=x^5/5-7x^4/4+14x^3/3-4x^2.

The phase y^2+f(x) is of class (W) with (a,b)=(5,2). Its four critical values are

0, -53/60, -4/15, -128/15.

They are distinct and their twelve ordered nonzero differences are pairwise distinct, although (Q) fails because the differences are rational. The audited homogeneous-group argument still applies with this finite distinct-difference property. This observation is about the homogeneous group; no chain or boundary-field comparison for this additional phase is asserted here.

The current candidate's hypothesis (Q) is sufficient and may be retained. The necessary editorial correction is simply not to infer its necessity or infer a smaller group merely from its failure. The frozen theorem has not been weakened or otherwise modified.
