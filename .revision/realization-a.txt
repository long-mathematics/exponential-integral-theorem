
\begin{externalinput}[Ordinary Nori structure]\label{input:ordinary}
We use the rational perverse-Nori categories of Ivorra--Morel, their
six operations and tensor structure, and their exact faithful perverse
Betti realization \cite{IvorraMorel,Terenzi,Tubach}. Write
\[
 \mathsf{DM}_c(X)=\mathsf{DM}_{\mathrm{et},c}(X,\Q),\qquad
 \mathsf{DN}(X)=\operatorname{Ind}D^b(\MN_\Q(X)).
\]
Tubach constructs the operation-compatible enhanced realization
$\mathsf{Nor}_X^*:\mathsf{DM}_c(X)\to D^b(\MN_\Q(X))$;
its zeroth perverse cohomology is the universal homological functor
$H_X$ defining the Nori heart. These are Theorem~4.5 and
Corollary~4.20 of \cite{Tubach}. All Tubach pinpoints in this paper
use arXiv~v4 numbering; the published numbering differs.

We also use the ordinary structural presentation
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nori-module-model}
 \mathsf{DN}(X)\simeq
 \operatorname{Mod}_{\mathscr N_X}(\mathsf{DM}_{\mathrm{et}}(X,\Q)),
 \qquad \mathscr N_X=a_X^*\mathscr N_k,
\end{equation}
where $\mathscr N_k=\mathsf{Nor}_{k,*}\one$. The algebra pullback
and module presentation are Lemma~4.12, Corollary~4.14, and
Proposition~4.16 of \cite{Tubach}; compact generation by smooth
geometric motives is Corollary~4.19. We use this for ordinary
structural and generation arguments, not to construct the algebraic
realization by an unproved de Rham augmentation.
\end{externalinput}

\subsection{Arithmetic mixed sheaves and their differential-module component}

Throughout this construction $k=\overline\Q\subset\C$ with its fixed
embedding, and coefficients are rational until the last paragraph of
\cref{thm:algebraic-interface}. Put
\[
 \mathcal H_k(X)=\MHM(X/k),\qquad
 D_k(X)=D^b(\mathcal H_k(X)).
\]
Here $\MHM(X/k)$ means Saito's category in \S1.8(ii) of
\cite{SaitoMixed}: a mixed Hodge module on $X_\C$ together with a
model over $k$ of its weight-filtered, Hodge-filtered algebraic
$D$-module. The polarizations on the weight gradeds are also defined
on that model. This is additional structure carried by the object and
its morphisms, not an objectwise choice of descent after realization.
There are exact faithful heart functors
\[
 q_X:\mathcal H_k(X)\longrightarrow\Perv(X(\C),\Q),\qquad
 d_X:\mathcal H_k(X)\longrightarrow\Hol_{\mathrm{reg}}(X/k).
\]
Their complex de Rham--Betti comparison is part of the object. For
singular varieties the differential-module notation uses local smooth
ambient varieties and Kashiwara equivalence. Derived functors use the
sheaf-compatible normalization fixed in \cref{sec:normalization}.

\begin{externalinput}[Saito's arithmetic mixed-sheaf formalism]
\label{input:arithmetic-mixed-sheaves}
The category just described is a theory of mixed sheaves in the sense of
\cite[\S\S1.1--1.8(ii)]{SaitoMixed}, extended to separated varieties
by Proposition~6.15 there. The derived operations, adjunction,
composition, base-change, external-product and duality maps are the
constructions of \S\S2--4; ordinary nearby and vanishing cycles are
constructed in \S5. They are compatible with $q_X$. This is a
triangulated mixed-sheaf input. Its enhancement and the verification
for its algebraic differential-module component are supplied below,
not attributed verbatim to Saito or to Tubach's complex-Hodge theorem.
\end{externalinput}

\begin{lemma}[The algebraic differential-module component]
\label{lem:arithmetic-D-operations}
The derived functors $d_X$ carry Saito's operations and their structural
maps to the corresponding algebraic differential-module operations
over $k$, including the ordinary geometric cycle maps. These
identifications retain the actual complex comparison.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the defining filtered modules, before forgetting their filtrations.
For a closed immersion the direct image is computed by the transfer
bimodule; for a smooth projection the direct image is the relative de
Rham complex, equivalently its Spencer resolution. Smooth pullback and
external product are the corresponding algebraic pullback and tensor
complexes. Their restriction, trace and product maps are defined over
$k$. For an open immersion with locally principal complement, the
filtered localization is defined with the algebraic $V$-filtration.
Saito's remark following \S1.8 explains that this filtration and its
intersection with the Hodge filtration commute with faithful scalar
extension to $\C$. Forgetting the filtrations therefore gives the usual
algebraic localization and its maps.

These are the primitive constructions used in Saito's \S\S2--4.
Finite affine Cech complexes, resolutions and their common refinements
assemble general direct images and restrictions from them. On forgetting
the filtrations these are the same complexes and the same refinement
maps used for algebraic differential modules. Duality is the algebraic
dual transfer construction; it gives the exceptional operations.
Consequently the adjunction units and counits, composition maps,
projection maps and base-change maps are identified before passing to
cohomology. The comparison with Betti complexes is the comparison of
these constructions, including their trace normalization, not a newly
chosen isomorphism between their outputs.

The same observation applies to the $V$-filtration construction of
ordinary cycles and its canonical and variation maps. Equivalently,
unipotent cycles can be obtained from the stabilized logarithmic gluing
diagrams of $j_!,j_*$ and the nilpotent logarithm objects. All these
objects and maps have their algebraic models over $k$. We shall only
use the other eigenparts for geometric, hence quasi-unipotent, input;
finite Kummer pullbacks and projectors supply those parts. No assertion
about arbitrary irregular moderate cycles follows from this lemma.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Transfer of the constructible-heart enhancement]
\label{prop:arithmetic-enhancement}
The categories $D_k(X)$ admit the constructible-heart enhancement and
form, after Ind-completion, a cocomplete coefficient system. Their
rational forgetful functors form an enhanced comparison with Betti
complexes. There is a symmetric monoidal realization
\[
 \operatorname{Hdg}_{k,X}^*:\mathsf{DM}_c(X)\longrightarrow D_k(X)
\]
compatible with the constructible six operations and their maps, with
$q_X\operatorname{Hdg}_{k,X}^*\simeq B_X$, the geometric Betti
realization. Its differential-module component computes geometric
algebraic de Rham complexes over $k$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
We verify the applicability of Tubach's construction rather than assuming
that an arbitrary triangulated formalism is already enhanced.

\emph{Triangulated hypotheses.}
Saito's adjunction, composition, base-change and external-product
constructions give the rational symmetric monoidal stable homotopy
$2$-functor used in the hypothesis of \cite[\S2]{Tubach}.
Here smooth purity for a smooth morphism of relative dimension $r$
identifies $f^!$ with $f^*(r)[2r]$; hence
$f_\sharp=f_!(r)[2r]$ is the left adjoint of $f^*$.
The localization triangles, smooth base change and their compatibility
with products are Saito's actual maps. The affine-line unit and the
projective-line trace are those of the constant mixed sheaf. Under $q$
they are the usual homotopy-invariance and Tate-purity maps; conservativity
therefore verifies the required invariance and stability. This uses
existing maps, not an inference that a conservative functor creates them.

\emph{Constructible hearts.}
The perverse $t$-structure and $q$ satisfy Tubach's stated hypotheses.
The constructible $t$-structure is obtained by the stratumwise perverse
shifts and gluing; $q$ is exact and conservative for it as well.
At a point, $D_k(k)$ is by definition the bounded derived category of
its heart. The ingredients of the effacement argument in Tubach's
\S2 are unchanged: affine vanishing is reflected by $q$; localization
and the affine-line projection are available with their adjunctions;
and objects are lisse on a finite stratification. On a smooth lisse
stratum their normalized tensor dual is given by the algebraic dual
connection and the dual variation. The actual evaluation and
coevaluation maps are isomorphisms where required because $q$ detects
this. Thus the lisse objects used in the argument are dualizable.
The induction on strata and the affine-space effacement in \S\S2.1--2.3
use precisely these facts and the point case, not an equivalence of the
point category with complex mixed Hodge structures. They give
\begin{equation}\label{eq:arithmetic-constructible-heart}
 D^b(\mathcal H_{k,\mathrm{ct}}(X))\simeq D_k(X)
\end{equation}
by the proof of \cite[Corollary~2.19]{Tubach}.

\emph{Enhancement and comparison.}
Pullbacks and tensor products on $\mathcal H_{k,\mathrm{ct}}$ are
exact, since their Betti counterparts are exact and $q$ reflects
cohomological concentration. Their composition, symmetry, unit and
associativity constraints are the mixed-sheaf constraints. Apply
\cite[Theorem~3.3 and Proposition~3.5]{Tubach} to this diagram of
abelian categories and exact functors, and then use
\eqref{eq:arithmetic-constructible-heart}. This constructs the enhanced
pullback and tensor diagram. Closed direct image, shifted smooth pullback
and Verdier duality have their exact perverse-heart descriptions and
therefore their derived enhancements. Adjoints and the factorization of
morphisms give the remaining operations, as in \S3.2 of that paper.
Applying the same construction to $q$ gives its enhanced comparison;
the exchange maps become the Betti exchange maps and hence are
isomorphisms. Coherence here comes from the exact-heart diagram and
adjunction mates. It is not deduced from faithfulness on a derived
category, which is not asserted.

\emph{Descent.}
The proof of \cite[Theorem~3.24]{Tubach} applies with $q$ as conservative
realization. In that proof one first works in a bounded constructible
amplitude. The mapping-space truncation in that bounded range reduces
the limits needed in the hypercover argument to finite stages; these
exist in the derived categories and are respected by the exact
realization. Conservativity and Betti descent identify the descent
maps. One then passes through bounded amplitudes; localization and
proper base change give the stated descent. We do not interchange
Ind-completion with an arbitrary infinite limit. All the operations,
$t$-structures and boundedness statements used here were supplied above.

\emph{Motivic factorization.}
These checks give the coefficient system required in
\cite[Definitions~7.5, 7.7 and Theorem~7.14]{DrewGallauer}.
Initiality supplies the map from stable motivic homotopy theory.
Rationalization and the plus/etale factorization are those in the proof
of \cite[Theorem~4.5 and Lemma~4.7]{Tubach}: the rational minus part
vanishes (our $k$ already contains a square root of $-1$), and etale
descent supplies the etale factor. This gives
$\operatorname{Hdg}_k^*$ and its Betti comparison.
Compatibility with all six operations on constructibles is a separate
step: restrict to the geometric image and apply the constructible
premotivic comparison argument in the proof of Tubach's Theorem~4.5.
The target is separated by descent and generated there by Tate-twisted
smooth images; the source has absolute purity and invertible Tate
objects. These are the hypotheses used by that argument to identify the
direct-image and exceptional exchange maps. Initiality alone is not
being used to assert compatibility with every right adjoint.
Finally \cref{lem:arithmetic-D-operations} identifies the resulting
geometric complexes and their comparison over $k$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Universal hearts and the tensor comparison}

\begin{lemma}[The arithmetic realization on Nori hearts]
\label{lem:arithmetic-heart}
There is an exact faithful functor
\[
 F_X:\MN_\Q(X)\longrightarrow\mathcal H_k(X),\qquad
 F_XH_X\simeq\pH^0\operatorname{Hdg}_{k,X}^*,\qquad
 q_XF_X\simeq\rat_X.
\]
Its comparisons are those induced by the universal abelian factorization.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Set $\beta_X=\pH^0B_X$ and
$h_X=\pH^0\operatorname{Hdg}_{k,X}^*$. On the universal abelian
envelope of the homological diagram $\mathsf{DM}_c(X)$ these extend
to exact functors $\overline\beta_X$ and $\overline h_X$.
The natural equality $q_X\overline h_X\simeq\overline\beta_X$ and
exact faithfulness of $q_X$ imply equality of their Serre kernels.
Consequently $\overline h_X$ descends through the quotient defining
$\MN_\Q(X)$, giving $F_X$. The displayed Betti comparison proves
faithfulness. This is a statement about the kernel on the abelian
envelope, not just zero objects of the original triangulated diagram.

We use functoriality of this construction for compatible exact morphisms
of representations and their transformations, including the opposite
categories for contravariant functors; see
\cite[\S\S2--3]{TerenziFunctoriality}. In several variables the relevant
version requires a functor exact in each variable, as in Propositions~4.3
and~4.6 there. No lifting statement for an arbitrary biadditive functor
is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[External products via perverse-concentrated generators]
\label{lem:perverse-tensor-lift}
The functors $F_X$ have natural external-product comparisons
\[
 F_X(M)\boxtimes F_Y(N)\simeq F_{X\times Y}(M\boxtimes N),
\]
compatible with Betti realization and with associativity and symmetry.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Use the full additive subcategory
\[
 \mathcal D^0(X)=\{A\in\mathsf{DM}_c(X):B_X(A)\in\Perv(X(\C),\Q)\}.
\]
By \cite[Theorem~1.12]{Terenzi}, the universal abelian factorization of
$B_X|_{\mathcal D^0(X)}$ is equivalent to $\MN_\Q(X)$. On this
subcategory $\operatorname{Hdg}_{k,X}^*(A)$ lies in the perverse heart,
since its Betti realization does and $q_X$ is exact conservative.
Moreover $\mathcal D^0(X)\boxtimes\mathcal D^0(Y)\subset
\mathcal D^0(X\times Y)$, by exactness of external product on
perverse sheaves. Thus the actual geometric tensor comparison is a
comparison inside the hearts on these diagrams.

For completeness one can first form the universal abelian factorization
of the heart-valued Hodge diagram. Its Serre kernel equals that of its
Betti composite by faithfulness of $q$, just as in
\cref{lem:arithmetic-heart}. The external product is exact separately
on the target hearts. The multi-exact universal lifting of
\cite[Proposition~4.3, Corollary~4.5 and Proposition~4.6]{TerenziFunctoriality}
therefore lifts this product and its comparison. Transporting along the
equal-kernel equivalence and Terenzi's equivalence gives the displayed
map. Naturality and the associativity and symmetry diagrams are preserved
by that lifting. Equivalently the resulting equalities can be checked by
the faithful Betti functor on the abelian target hearts.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why the concentrated presentation matters]
For arbitrary complexes one cannot replace
$\pH^0(A\boxtimes B)$ by $\pH^0(A)\boxtimes\pH^0(B)$.
Already at a point, $A=\Q[1]$ and $B=\Q[-1]$ have zero zeroth
cohomology, whereas $H^0(A\otimes B)=\Q$. The preceding proof
uses perverse-concentrated generators before applying the multi-exact
universal property; it does not treat external product as a unary
$t$-exact operation on arbitrary complexes.
\end{remark}
