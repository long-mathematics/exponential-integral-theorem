
\subsection{Operation maps, enhancement, and absolute normalization}

\begin{proposition}[Arithmetic Nori realization and its operations]
\label{prop:arithmetic-Nori-operations}
The derived functors $R_{H,k,X}=D^b(F_X)$ assemble into a symmetric
monoidal enhanced realization, compatible with the six operations,
ordinary geometric nearby and vanishing cycles, and their maps.
They satisfy
\[
 q_XR_{H,k,X}\simeq R_{B,X},\qquad
 R_{H,k,X}\mathsf{Nor}_X^*\simeq\operatorname{Hdg}_{k,X}^*.
\]
After forgetting the model over $k$, they agree with the established
Nori-to-Hodge realization and its comparison.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
We first construct the comparison maps, and then verify their enhanced
coherence. For a closed immersion $i$, a smooth morphism $g$ of relative
dimension $r$, or Verdier duality, use respectively $i_*$, $g^*[r]$, or
$\DD$. These are exact on the perverse hearts, with the contravariant
interpretation for duality. Denote one of them by $h$. Tubach's
$\mathsf{Nor}^*$ commutes with the derived operation, so
\[
 h_NH_X\simeq H_Yh_{\mathsf{DM}}.
\]
The corresponding geometric identity for $\operatorname{Hdg}_k^*$
gives a comparison of the compatible exact representations
\[
 F_Yh_NH_X\simeq h_HF_XH_X.
\]
The universal functoriality used in \cref{lem:arithmetic-heart} lifts
it to $F_Yh_N\simeq h_HF_X$. The lifted comparison is the Betti one
under $q$. Deriving the exact functors gives the corresponding
comparison of enhanced derived functors. The external product is
handled separately by \cref{lem:perverse-tensor-lift}, not by this
unary argument.

For a functor whose comparison has been constructed, form the
adjunction mate for its left or right adjoint. Under the enhanced Betti
comparison it is exactly the Betti mate, which is an isomorphism.
Conservativity of $q$ therefore makes the constructed mate an
isomorphism. In particular this gives $i^*$ from $i_*$, and general
pullback by factoring a quasi-projective morphism as a closed immersion
followed by a smooth morphism. It then gives $f_*$ and smooth
$f_\sharp$ from pullback. Duality gives $f_!,f^!$. The internal tensor
comparison is the diagonal pullback of the external one, and internal
Hom follows by its adjunction. The unit comparison at a point is the
one on the geometric unit in the universal diagram; pullback gives it
on every base. These constructions identify actual adjunction and
exchange maps, not just the isomorphism classes of their values.

For the coherence argument restrict $R_{H,k,X}$ to constructible
hearts. The Betti comparison shows that this restriction is exact and
that the pullback and tensor comparisons just obtained are comparisons
of exact heart functors. All composition, associativity, symmetry and
unit diagrams there commute: the exact conservative realization to
constructible sheaves is faithful on the heart and identifies the
diagrams with the Betti diagrams. Applying
\cite[Theorem~3.3 and Proposition~3.5]{Tubach} to the entire diagram
gives its symmetric monoidal enhanced natural transformation. Under
the constructible-heart derived equivalences it is the preceding
$D^b(F_X)$, since both are derived from the same exact-heart functor.
Adjunction mates supply the enhanced operation coherences. No equality
of arbitrary derived morphisms is inferred from conservativity alone.

The composite $R_{H,k}\mathsf{Nor}^*$ is now a morphism of coefficient
systems. The same uniqueness used in the proof of
\cite[Corollary~4.8]{Tubach}, applied to the target constructed in
\cref{prop:arithmetic-enhancement}, identifies it with
$\operatorname{Hdg}_k^*$. This is an identification with Tubach's
universal realization, not an assumed identification with a separately
constructed cellular functor. Forgetting the arithmetic model and using
the universal heart diagrams similarly recovers the established
Nori-to-Hodge realization.

Finally, use the actual logarithmic gluing construction of unipotent
cycles in \cite[\S3.5, Corollary~3.14]{IvorraMorel} and
\cite[\S5]{SaitoMixed}. The logarithm objects on $\Gm$, their nilpotent
endomorphisms, and their restriction and trace maps are geometric
over $k$. The realization carries them to the corresponding logarithmic
connections. It commutes with $j_!,j_*$, their comparison, and the
finite stabilized kernels and cokernels defining cycles; hence also
with the canonical and variation maps. For the geometric input needed
here, monodromy is quasi-unipotent. Tensoring with finite Kummer
objects and taking character projectors reduces every other eigenpart
to the unipotent part. These projectors are treated over finite
coefficient extensions before extending coefficients to $k$. This proves
the cycle assertion in its stated geometric scope.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The actual absolute de Rham comparison]
\label{lem:absolute-dR-comparison}
At $\Spec k$, the functor $d_kR_{H,k,k}$ is the usual absolute Nori
de Rham realization. Its comparison with the Betti fiber is the
geometric integration comparison, including the product and Tate
normalizations.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
The absolute perverse-Nori heart is the usual category of Nori motives;
we use the good-pair presentation recalled in
\cite[\S1.2]{Tubach}. For a geometric pair, apply its relative
localization triangle and absolute direct image to the constant object.
By \cref{prop:arithmetic-enhancement,lem:arithmetic-D-operations}, the
differential-module component is its algebraic relative de Rham complex,
and the rational component is its relative Betti complex. Their
comparison is algebraic de Rham integration. For a good pair, take the
single designated cohomology group. This identifies its image with the
usual de Rham realization of that Nori generator.

Morphisms of pairs, connecting maps for triples, products, and the Tate
map are the maps of these relative complexes. The realization already
commutes with those operations and maps, so the identifications just
obtained are compatible with the entire good-pair diagram, not only
with its vector spaces. In cohomological variance the Tate period is
$2\pi i$; in the dual homological presentation use the dual comparison
on both sides. The universal abelian factorization extends the
identification and its comparison to all absolute Nori objects and
morphisms. The multi-exact product construction gives the tensor
compatibility. Thus the absolute comparison is the specified one.
This argument identifies heart realizations from their geometric
presentation; it does not require an enhanced comparison between the
cellular and universal constructions of $\mathsf{Nor}^*$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Algebraic realization and comparison interface]
\label{thm:algebraic-interface}
For every quasi-projective $X/k$ set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:relative-algebraic-realization}
 R_{\dR,X}=d_XR_{H,k,X}:D^b(\MN_\Q(X))
       \longrightarrow D^b_{\mathrm{reg,hol}}(X/k).
\end{equation}
This realization has the following properties on constructible objects.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.5em]
\item It is symmetric monoidal and compatible with the six operations
and their adjunction, projection and base-change maps. Its composite
with $\mathsf{Nor}_X^*$ is the geometric algebraic de Rham realization.
\item After $k\hookrightarrow\C$, regular Riemann--Hilbert identifies
it naturally with complexified Betti realization. Its complex algebraic
$D$-module is the underlying module of the established Nori-to-Hodge
realization, with the actual comparison.
\item It is $t$-exact for the perverse and holonomic hearts and exact
faithful on those hearts. It commutes with ordinary geometric nearby
and vanishing cycles and their canonical and variation maps.
\item Its absolute restriction is the usual de Rham fiber $d_k$, with
the actual absolute comparison and its tensor and Tate normalizations.
\end{enumerate}
The same conclusions hold after the $k$-linear coefficient extension
of the rational Nori category, separately from geometric base extension.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Compose \cref{prop:arithmetic-Nori-operations} with
\cref{lem:arithmetic-D-operations}. The complex comparison is part of
Saito's arithmetic object, and is natural for the constructed operations.
Exactness of the two heart functors gives $t$-exactness. If the algebraic
realization of a heart object vanishes, its complex Betti realization
vanishes, hence the object vanishes. Applying zero-object detection to
images of morphisms proves faithfulness. Bounded cohomology then gives
derived conservativity. The absolute statement is
\cref{lem:absolute-dR-comparison}.

For a finite coefficient field $E/\Q$ inside $k$, first realize the
rational object with its $E$-action, and then take the summand for the
specified embedding in $E\otimes_\Q k$. Separability makes this
projector exact. Finite diagrams in the $k$-linear coefficient extension
are defined over some such $E$, followed by idempotent completion.
The realizations, tensor comparisons and operation maps commute with
these projectors. Their filtered union gives the stated coefficient
extension; no geometric extension to $\C$ is being confused with it.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Scope of the construction]\label{rem:interface-discharge}
The construction passes through arithmetic mixed sheaves, their
constructible-heart enhancement, and the universal Nori heart. It does
not assume a coefficient system of all algebraic $D$-modules over $k$,
nor obtain a de Rham augmentation from an unproved comparison of two
enhanced Nori functors. The ordinary module presentation
\eqref{eq:nori-module-model} remains available but is not an input to
\eqref{eq:relative-algebraic-realization}. The absolute normalization is
proved separately because an abstract fiber-functor comparison alone
would not identify the period functions used later. The transfer of
Saito's arithmetic theory through Tubach's method is the proposition
proved above, not a claim that Tubach stated this arithmetic version.
\end{remark}
